SoR: video games,
a modest proposal

Hello, School of Rock instructors, coordinators, show directors and students!

I was having a chat with Matt during band practice, and he mentioned that there's never been a season with a video game theme. As someone who's familiarity with music almost solely comes from its proximity to gaming, I personally think there is a huge missed opportunity here. I slid the idea by several different people, students and instructors alike, and their immediate reaction is usually: "hmmm, there's no vocals in video game music, right?" or "isn't it just blips and bloops?"

Well...

Below is a curated playlist of a potential gaming-themed season (and then some). Every single song was considered from the perspective of: "would this work in a School of Rock setting?", i.e. difficulty, instrument representation, PG-13 lyrics. Since most songs are not on the Method app, other sources of sheet music and tabs are listed.

Why the website? When you inevitably wonder "how is this from a video game?", come back here. This full page is not meant to be read in its entirety in one sitting (though you can, if you want). Listen to the playlist first, return here for some education.

Whether this actually becomes a full season unto itself, a few songs are used for a different season, you save one or two songs to your own personal playlists, or you just learn something new, I hope you all enjoy.

- Lu

The Trojan Horse(s)

These songs are going undercover. They're normal chart-topping songs that you wouldn't expect to be associated with video games, but somehow, each found an important place in a major game or game adaptation. They show how licensed music can shape a scene, deepen a story, and become permanently associated with a gaming experience.
