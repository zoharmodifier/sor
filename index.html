Hello, School of Rock instructors, coordinators, and show directors!

I was having a conversation with Matt during band practice one day, and he told me that there’s never been a season with a video game theme. As someone who’s familiarity with music almost solely comes from its proximity to gaming, I personally think there is a huge missed opportunity here.

Last season, I nonchalantly gave Matt a song request, and that didn’t work at all. This time, I am doubling down and taking the exact opposite approach: I detailed out what a whole season could potentially look like.

Below is a curated playlist and an accompanying brief history behind each song. TO BE CLEAR: this is not a list of "songs I personally like", but rather, each song was considered from the perspective of: "would this work in a School of Rock setting?" (Difficulty, instrument representation, PG-13 lyrics). I've also included sheet music and tabs for all songs, where possible.

Hope you all enjoy.

- Lu

The Trojan Horse(s)

These songs were selected with the precise reason of: they're songs you wouldn't expect to be associated with video games. But somehow, each found an important place in a major game or game adaptation. They show how licensed music can shape a scene, deepen a story, and become permanently associated with a gaming experience.

Artist

Origin
Twisted Metal: Black

Twisted Metal: Black was an early example of a game using licensed mainstream music to establish its identity. “Paint It, Black” gave the game an immediate sense of darkness and familiarity, and its availability in the School of Rock Method app makes it especially practical for this season.
